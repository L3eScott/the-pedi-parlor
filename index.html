What We Offer

Gentle Services, beautifully done

Every visit is unhurried and hygienic, with fresh tools and a warm, respectful touch — designed especially for seniors and anyone who prefers care at home.

Signature Pedi Parlor

Our full experience: warm soak, gentle nail & cuticle care, callus smoothing, a soothing foot & lower-leg massage, and a polish of your choice.

Gentle Nail Trim & File

Careful trimming, shaping, and smoothing for toenails and fingernails — quick, comfortable, and perfect for regular upkeep between visits.

Diabetic-Friendly Footcare

Extra-gentle, low-risk nail and skin care with careful attention and clean technique, for sensitive feet that deserve a soft touch.

Classic Manicure

Soften, shape, and polish — a little hand pampering that leaves you feeling put-together and cared for.

Mani + Pedi Duo

The complete treat: hands and feet in one relaxing at-home session. Our most-loved way to feel fully pampered.

Community & Facility Visits

Group pampering days for senior living communities and care homes. Bring joy and self-care to your residents — we handle everything.
